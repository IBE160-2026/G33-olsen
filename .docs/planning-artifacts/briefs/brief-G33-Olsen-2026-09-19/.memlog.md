Expiry is a secondary consideration. Supplier lead-time uncertainty matters; the product should record order-sent and delivery-received dates across many suppliers to make lead-time performance visible.
- (decision) Decision-support principle: show all relevant information without having the system decide what is critical. Use a priority list to guide attention, ordered as immediate shortage warning first, then recommended order quantity/latest safe order date, then supplier comparison. User controls whether to drill into recommendations or comparisons.

- (decision) Version-one success criteria: correctly recalculate ingredient and packaging requirements after a production-plan change; immediately reveal likely shortages; recommend order quantity and latest safe order date; show recorded supplier delivery performance; and have AI explain why each warning or recommendation appears.

- (decision) Add version-one descriptive analytics: show volume trends that are increasing or decreasing. This is historical/statistical visibility, not advanced demand forecasting. Version-one exclusions confirmed: real ERP/supplier integrations, automated supplier ordering, advanced forecasting, full production scheduling/capacity optimization, real company data, and enterprise-grade security.
- (decision) Analytics scope confirmed: production/demand volume trends by product, inventory movement or consumption by material, and supplier delivery performance including average lead time and late deliveries.
